Assessment that knows the learner: formative practice, AI and automation in school education.

I am a doctoral researcher (UGC-JRF) in the Department of Education and Education Technology, School of Social Sciences, University of Hyderabad, working under the supervision of Prof. J. V. Research

Research lines

Three strands of work, from my doctoral study to AI-supported assessment and early childhood education.

Doctoral study Design-based research · Supervisor: Prof. J. V. Madhusudan

Automated formative assessment in school education

How can automated and data-driven assessment strengthen teachers' formative practice without losing sight of the learner? The study designs and evaluates automated formative assessment that draws on multidimensional learner data.

  • Formative assessment
  • Educational data mining
  • Teacher roles
  • Personalised learning
  • Affective readiness
Proposed Research synopsis

Physics-grounded multimodal AI for assessing handwritten responses

A neuro-symbolic approach to assessing students' handwritten Class 11 mechanics answers. A multimodal large language model reads the written work and diagrams, and symbolic physics constraints check whether the reasoning holds. The aim is assessment that is both scalable and faithful to the physics.

  • Physics education
  • Multimodal LLMs
  • Neuro-symbolic AI
  • Automated scoring
Ongoing study Early childhood education

Samskara formation and holistic development in early childhood

A comparative study of Shishuvatika with Froebel, Montessori and Anganwadi approaches. It examines how each contributes to the holistic development of the young child, how parents perceive संस्कार (samskara) formation, and how teachers integrate it into everyday pedagogy. A companion concept article maps ज्ञानज, कर्मज and भावज संस्कार to the पंचकोश framework for teacher education.

  • Early childhood education
  • Shishuvatika
  • Holistic development
  • Indian knowledge systems
